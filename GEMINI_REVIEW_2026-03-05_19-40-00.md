## 3. General Observations & Guidance

### [LOW] Observation: Thread Management
- **Note:** The retention scheduler uses a daemon thread. While appropriate for this MVP's local Docker deployment, ensure that any future migration to a production-scale environment (e.g., Kubernetes) replaces this with a proper cron-job or distributed task scheduler (Celery Beat).

### [LOW] Observation: Exception Masking in Retention
- **Note:** In `_retention_loop`, exceptions are caught and ignored to prevent thread death. This is acceptable, but consider adding a log entry (once logging is implemented) to ensure that persistent retention failures are visible to operators.
