## 1. Executive Summary
The implementation of the **OpenAI and Google Provider Adapters (T2.2a/b)** is of high quality. The normalization of evidence signals into a standard schema provides a stable foundation for the upcoming media understanding and extraction phases.

## 2. Review Findings & Verification

### [PASSED] 1. Architecture & Strategy Pattern
- **Verification:** The `ProviderAdapter` base class and its implementation in `OpenAIAdapter` and `GoogleAdapter` correctly encapsulate provider-specific model plans, cost estimation, and transcription logic.
- **Factory Pattern:** The `get_provider_adapter` factory correctly resolves the selection at runtime based on the job metadata.

### [PASSED] 2. Schema Normalization
- **Verification:** The `EvidencePayload` ensures that `transcript_text`, `visual_events`, and `process_candidates` follow a consistent structure. This is critical for `T2.3` and `T2.4` where downstream agents will process these signals without needing to know their origin.

### [PASSED] 3. Fallback Orchestration
- **Verification:** `worker.py` now includes a robust `try...except` block around the primary adapter execution. It correctly switches to the alternative provider if the primary fails, fulfilling the resiliency requirement in the PRD.

### [PASSED] 4. Integration Integrity
- **Verification:** The worker now correctly updates the `JobRecord` with the resolved `model_plan`, `usage_cost_estimate`, and granular `progress` (including the normalized evidence).

---

## 3. General Observations & Guidance

### [MEDIUM] Observation: Error Granularity in Fallback
- **Note:** Currently, the worker triggers a fallback on *any* exception from the primary adapter. For production readiness, it is recommended to distinguish between "retriable" errors (e.g., rate limits, 503s) and "fatal" errors (e.g., invalid input, auth failure). For the MVP, the current broad catch is acceptable.

### [LOW] Observation: Manual Dictionary Conversion
- **Note:** In `worker.py`, the `EvidencePayload` object is manually converted to a dictionary for DB persistence. To improve maintainability, consider using `dataclasses.asdict()` or implementing a `.to_dict()` method on the payload classes.

## 4. Approval for Progression
The project is cleared to proceed to **Milestone T2.3 (Media Understanding Pipeline)** and **T2.4 (Process Extraction Pipeline)**.

**Guidance for T2.3/T2.4:**
- **Signal Merging:** As you move into `T2.4`, focus on the "Merging Signals" requirement. Handling overlap between visual cues and spoken steps will be the primary challenge.
- **Confidence Scoring:** Ensure that the `confidence` score in the evidence payload starts being derived from actual AI provider confidence markers where available.
