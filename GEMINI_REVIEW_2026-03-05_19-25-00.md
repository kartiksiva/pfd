## 3. General Observations & Guidance

### [MEDIUM] Observation: Synchronous Finalization
- **Note:** `finalize_job` currently runs `generate_exports` synchronously within the request. While fine for the current volume and document size, if PDF generation becomes significantly slower (e.g., due to complex images/tables), this should be moved to a background task to avoid client timeouts.

### [LOW] Observation: Database Migration Consistency
- **Note:** While `Base.metadata.create_all` handles fresh installs, `ensure_schema_compat` in `database.py` is the safety net for iterative SQLite updates. Ensure that every new column added to `JobRecord` is mirrored in `ensure_schema_compat` for consistent dev environment upgrades.
